# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Local Lowcountry homeowners (Bluffton, Hilton Head, Okatie, Sun City, Savannah area) who need carpet, upholstery, rug, or mattress cleaning. Primary job: land on the site, quickly understand what Heather does and that she can be trusted, then book a time or call/text her directly. They are not comparison-shopping extensively — they want a local person they feel good about.

## Product Purpose

Swanson Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning is Heather Swanson's sole-proprietor cleaning business. The site exists to establish trust, present services clearly, and convert visitors into bookings (via Calendly) or direct calls. Success means a visitor books Heather before leaving the page.

## Brand Personality

Warm, personal, and rooted in the Lowcountry. Heather is a trusted neighbor, not a franchise. Voice is friendly and direct — confident without being corporate. Three words: **warm, trustworthy, local**.

## Anti-references

- Big-chain franchise cleaning brands (ServiceMaster, SERVPRO) — loud, generic, heavy on red/orange urgency badges, impersonal
- Cold or clinical — medical-white, zero warmth, feels like a hospital not a home service

## Design Principles

1. **Heather is the brand** — Personal touches, her name and face, and direct language outweigh any generic "we" copy or stock-photo polish.
2. **Trust before the fold** — Social proof, credentials, and the phone number should be visible before a visitor has to scroll.
3. **One clear next step** — Every section should funnel toward booking or calling. Remove anything that competes with that.
4. **Coastal warmth, not beach kitsch** — The teal/cream/gold palette should feel like a well-kept Lowcountry home, not a tourist shop.
5. **Simple enough for any device** — Many local homeowners are on phones. Speed and tap targets matter more than visual complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirements. Target WCAG AA as a baseline — good contrast, keyboard navigation, and usable on any device for a typical local audience.
