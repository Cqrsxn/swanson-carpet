Frequently Asked Questions

High heat extraction (hot water extraction / steam cleaning) injects hot water and cleaning solution deep into carpet fibers, then immediately extracts it along with dissolved dirt, allergens, and bacteria. The heat kills bacteria and dust mites that cold-water cleaning misses. It's considered the most effective carpet cleaning method by most manufacturers.

Typically 2–6 hours depending on carpet thickness, humidity, and airflow. We recommend opening windows, running fans, or turning on your AC after cleaning. On warm Lowcountry days with good ventilation, most carpets are walkable within a few hours.

Absolutely. We offer chemical-free cleaning using high heat and vapor steam, no chemicals needed at all. When we do use any solution, it's non-toxic and pet/family safe. Just let us know when you book if you have young children or sensitive pets.

We have excellent results with most stains, including old pet urine, red wine, coffee, and food stains. Some very old or dye-based stains may be permanent (like certain bleach stains or dye transfers), but we'll always give you an honest assessment before we start, with no surprise charges.
