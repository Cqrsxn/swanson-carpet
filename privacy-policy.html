Swanson Cleaning ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you share with us.

Information We Collect

Swanson Cleaning does not collect personal information through our website. To schedule an appointment, visitors contact Heather directly by phone or text — those conversations take place through your phone carrier, not through our website.

When you call or text to book, you may share information such as:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Address or service location

This information is collected for scheduling and communication purposes only.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share with us to:

  • Schedule cleaning service appointments
  • Contact you about your appointment
  • Confirm, reschedule, or follow up about a requested service
  • Respond to questions related to your booking

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information used for scheduling purposes. However, no website, online service, or electronic transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Swanson Cleaning does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Your Choices

You may choose not to share your name, address, or phone number with us. However, if you do not, we may not be able to schedule or confirm your cleaning appointment.

To update or remove information you've shared with us, please contact us using the contact information below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
